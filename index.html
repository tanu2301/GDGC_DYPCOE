What is GDSC?

Google Developer Student Clubs is a developer program for university students to learn mobile and web development skills, design thinking skills and leadership skills.

Concept of GDSC

The GDSC program is a grassroots channel through which Google provides development skills, mobile and web development skills for students, toward community building.

Target Audience

The GDSC program is for university students who are interested in learning mobile and web development skills, and want to connect with people who share their interests.

Why GDSC?

The GDSC program is a grassroots channel through which Google provides development skills and peer-to-peer learning opportunities.

Some Other Concept

The GDSC program is a grassroots channel through which Google provides development skills and community opportunities to students.
